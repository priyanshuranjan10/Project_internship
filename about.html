About UdaanFest

Every year, colleges across the city/state organise inter-college fests, sports meets, and cultural and technical events. Until now, registration for these events has been scattered across Google Forms, WhatsApp groups, and paper forms — making it hard for students to keep track of what they've signed up for, and hard for organisers to see who's coming.

UdaanFest is a centralised web portal where any student from an affiliated college can browse upcoming activities, register once with their personal and college details, and choose any number of Co-Curricular and Extra-Curricular activities they want to take part in. Organisers get a clean admin dashboard to see registrations per activity and per college.

What makes it different

  • Activities are read live from a database — the list can be updated any time without touching the HTML.
  • One registration record per student, linked to any number of activities through a proper many-to-many database design.
  • Server-side validation and duplicate-registration checks, on top of client-side JavaScript checks, keep the data clean and secure.
  • A simple student login lets participants review or edit their own registration later.

Who can register

Any student currently enrolled at a participating college or university within the city/state is welcome to register, regardless of course or year, as long as the chosen activities are open at the Inter-College level.
